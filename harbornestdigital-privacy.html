Privacy Policy

HarborNestDigital — Last updated: 23 October 2026

HarborNestDigital ("we", "us") sells digital planner products through our Etsy shop and markets those products on Pinterest. This policy explains what information our Pinterest application, "HarborNest Publisher", accesses and how that information is used.

What our application does

HarborNest Publisher is a private tool used only by HarborNestDigital to create and schedule Pins on HarborNestDigital's own Pinterest account, and to read that account's own boards and Pins so we can manage our published content. It is not offered to the public, and no other Pinterest user can connect to it or use it.

Information we access

Through the Pinterest API, using Pinterest's official OAuth authorisation, the application accesses only data belonging to the HarborNestDigital Pinterest account:

  • Account profile details for the connected account (user account read)
  • The account's own boards (read and write)
  • The account's own Pins (read and write)

How the information is used

The information is used solely to publish, schedule and review HarborNestDigital's own marketing Pins. We do not collect, read or store information about any other Pinterest user. We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. We do not use Pinterest data for advertising profiling, and we do not track visitors across websites.

Data storage and retention

OAuth access tokens are stored securely in our own systems and are used only to operate the application described above. We keep no database of Pinterest user data. If the application is disconnected, its access ends immediately and any stored tokens are deleted.

Cookies

This page and our application do not set cookies and do not run analytics or advertising trackers.

Your choices

The connected account owner can revoke the application's access at any time from Pinterest account settings, under Apps and authorised applications.
