About us

HarborNestDigital is a small digital products business based in Pakistan. We design and sell downloadable planner apps and spreadsheet tools through our Etsy shop. Our products are made for busy families, neurodivergent adults, students, homeschoolers and small sellers who want simple tools that work offline, with no subscription and no account required.

What we make

  • Life and family planners — ADHD life planner, mom and family organizer, homeschool planner
  • Money tools — ADHD budget planner, debt payoff and net worth trackers
  • Seller tools — reseller inventory and profit tracker
  • Care planners — pet care and medication planner

Shop: harbornestdigital.etsy.com

How we use Pinterest

We use Pinterest to share our own product designs and planner ideas with people looking for organization tools. Our developer app is used only to create and schedule Pins on our own Pinterest boards, for our own products, on our own account. It does not collect, sell or share anyone else's data.
